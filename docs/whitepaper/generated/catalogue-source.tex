\texttt{folder} & Every image in a directory, in sorted order, as a frame sequence & path, pattern='*', fps=30.0 \\
\texttt{image} & A single still image, as a one-frame sequence & path, fps=1.0 \\
\texttt{video} & Frames from a video file, plus the \texttt{fps} and \texttt{size} sinks need & path \\
